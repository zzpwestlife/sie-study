### Answers and explanations
### 答案与解析

1. **C.** ...getting a customer’s signature would be nearly impossible.
   **1. C.** ...获取客户签名几乎是不可能的。

2. **B.** The SEC is a government agency and is not a self-regulatory organization.
   **2. B.** SEC 是政府机构，不是自律组织。

3. **B.** ...at least quarterly (every three months)...
   **3. B.** ...至少每季度（每三个月）...

4. **A.** The ex-dividend date is one business day before the record date.
   **4. A.** 除息日是股权登记日之前的一个工作日。

5. **C.** Pump and dump is the only violation listed that is a form of market manipulation.
   **5. C.** 拉高出货是列出的唯一一种市场操纵形式。

6. **D.** All of the choices listed are subject to the FINRA 5 percent markup policy...
   **6. D.** 列出的所有选项均受 FINRA 5% 加价政策约束...

7. **D.** Certainly all of the choices listed...
   **7. D.** 当然，列出的所有选项...

8. **B.** ...felony conviction in the last 10 years, not 15.
   **8. B.** ...过去 10 年内的重罪定罪，而不是 15 年。

9. **D.** ...has up to two years to get registered...
   **9. D.** ...有两年的时间进行注册...

10. **D.** All of the choices listed are violations.
    **10. D.** 列出的所有选项均为违规行为。

11. **C.** Stock, corporate bond, and municipal bond transactions settle in two business days...
    **11. C.** 股票、公司债券和市政债券交易在两个工作日后结算...

12. **A.** Under Regulation S-P...
    **12. A.** 根据条例 S-P...

13. **B.** ...a markup or markdown does not need to be included for a principal transaction.
    **13. B.** ...对于委托交易，不需要包含加价或减价。

14. **B.** ...ex-dividend date... one business day before the record date — in this case, Thursday, October 7.
    **14. B.** ...除息日... 股权登记日之前的一个工作日 —— 在这种情况下是 10 月 7 日星期四。

15. **C.** Arbitration decisions are binding and non-appealable.
    **15. C.** 仲裁裁决具有约束力且不可上诉。

16. **D.** All of the choices listed are subject to the 5 percent markup policy.
    **16. D.** 列出的所有选项均受 5% 加价政策约束。

17. **A.** Ledgers... must be kept on file for a minimum of six years, not three.
    **17. A.** 分类账... 必须至少保存六年，而不是三年。

18. **B.** ...report the transaction(s) to the U.S. Treasury Financial Crimes Network (FinCEN).
    **18. B.** ...向美国财政部金融犯罪执法网络 (FinCEN) 报告交易。

19. **A.** The maximum penalties... are $5 million per individual... and up to 20 years in prison...
    **19. A.** 最高处罚... 个人 500 万美元... 以及最高 20 年监禁...

20. **D.** Pump and dump is a violation...
    **20. D.** 拉高出货是一种违规行为...

21. **A.** Freeriding is a violation...
    **21. A.** 搭便车是一种违规行为...

22. **B.** ...emergency contact information for two principals...
    **22. B.** ...两名主管的紧急联系信息...
