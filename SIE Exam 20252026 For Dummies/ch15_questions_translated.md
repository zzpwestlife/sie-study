
## 5. Testing Your Knowledge
## 5. 知识测试

### Practice questions
### 练习题

1. Which of the following are regressive taxes?
   I. Sales II. Income III. Gasoline IV. Alcohol
   (A) III and IV (B) I, II, and III (C) I, III, and IV (D) I, II, III, and IV

   **1. 以下哪些属于累退税？**
   I. 销售税 II. 所得税 III. 汽油税 IV. 酒精税
   (A) III 和 IV (B) I, II, 和 III (C) I, III, 和 IV (D) I, II, III, 和 IV

2. All of the following are types of tax-qualified retirement plans EXCEPT
   (A) 401(k) (B) profit-sharing (C) IRA (D) deferred compensation

   **2. 以下所有都是税务合格的退休计划类型，除了：**
   (A) 401(k) (B) 利润分享计划 (C) IRA (D) 递延薪酬计划

3. Which of the following are TRUE regarding Roth IRAs and Roth 401(k)s?
   (A) Withdrawals from both are tax-free provided that investors have held the accounts for at least five years and have reached the age of 59 1/2.
   (B) There are no contribution limits.
   (C) Contributions made to both are made pretax.
   (D) All of the above.

   **3. 关于 Roth IRA 和 Roth 401(k)，以下哪项是正确的？**
   (A) 如果投资者持有账户至少五年且年满 59.5 岁，两者的提款都是免税的。
   (B) 没有缴款限额。
   (C) 两者的缴款都是税前的。
   (D) 以上所有。

4. An investor buys 1,000 shares of a stock at $30. If the stock increases in value to $50, how would the result be categorized?
   (A) Profit (B) Appreciation (C) Capital gain (D) Investment income

   **4. 一位投资者以 $30 买入 1,000 股股票。如果股票价值涨至 $50，该结果应如何分类？**
   (A) 利润 (B) 增值 (Appreciation) (C) 资本利得 (D) 投资收入

5. According to the wash sale rule, if a customer sold a security at a loss, which of the following is TRUE?
   (A) The customer cannot purchase call options on the same security for 30 days before or after the sale and be able to claim the loss.
   (B) The customer cannot purchase bonds by the same issuer for 30 days before and after the sale and be able to claim the loss.
   (C) The customer cannot sell short the same security within 30 days before or after the sale and be able to claim the loss.
   (D) The customer cannot purchase mutual funds holding the same security for 30 days before and after the sale and be able to claim the loss.

   **5. 根据洗售规则（wash sale rule），如果客户亏损卖出证券，以下哪项是正确的？**
   (A) 客户在卖出前后 30 天内不得购买该证券的看涨期权，否则无法申报损失。
   (B) 客户在卖出前后 30 天内不得购买同一发行人的债券，否则无法申报损失。
   (C) 客户在卖出前后 30 天内不得卖空该证券，否则无法申报损失。
   (D) 客户在卖出前后 30 天内不得购买持有该证券的共同基金，否则无法申报损失。

### Answers and explanations
### 答案与解析

1. **C.** Sales, gasoline, and alcohol taxes are regressive... Income tax is progressive.
   **1. C.** 销售税、汽油税和酒精税是累退税... 所得税是累进税。

2. **D.** Deferred compensation plans are nonqualified...
   **2. D.** 递延薪酬计划是非合格计划...

3. **A.** Roth accounts offer tax-free withdrawals if held for 5 years and the investor is at least 59 1/2.
   **3. A.** 如果持有账户满 5 年且投资者年满 59.5 岁，Roth 账户提供免税提款。

4. **B.** Until the stock is sold, the increase in value is called appreciation (unrealized gain).
   **4. B.** 在股票卖出之前，价值的增加称为增值（未实现收益）。

5. **A.** Under the wash sale rule, you cannot buy the same security or anything convertible into it...
   **5. A.** 根据洗售规则，你不能在 30 天窗口期内购买相同的证券或任何可转换为该证券的资产...

6. **A.** An investor can always contribute money to an IRA even if covered by an employer pension plan. However, whether it’s deductible depends on the investor’s earnings... an investor who makes up to $77,000 can contribute to an IRA and be able to deduct the full amount...
   **6. A.** 即使参加了雇主养老金计划，投资者也总是可以向 IRA 缴款。但是，是否可抵税取决于投资者的收入... 收入在 $77,000 以下的投资者可以向 IRA 缴款并全额抵税...

7. **C.** Cash dividends, interest from corporate bonds, and interest from U.S. government bonds are all taxable... stock dividends are not taxable...
   **7. C.** 现金股息、公司债券利息和美国政府债券利息都需要纳税... 股票股息不纳税...

8. **A.** This is a short-term capital gain because when a security is sold up to and including one year from the purchase date... taxed at the investor’s tax bracket.
   **8. A.** 这是短期资本利得，因为证券在购买之日起一年内（含一年）卖出... 按投资者的税率档次征税。

9. **C.** 403(b) plans are set up for public school employees...
   **9. C.** 403(b) 计划是为公立学校员工设立的...
