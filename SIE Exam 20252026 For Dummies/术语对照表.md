# SIE 考试术语对照表 (Glossary)

本对照表基于 *SIE Exam 2025/2026 For Dummies* 内容整理，涵盖证券承销、监管法规、权益证券、债务证券、市政债及组合证券等核心术语。

| 英文术语 (English Term) | 中文翻译 (Chinese Translation) | 专业定义 (Definition) | 上下文示例 (Context Example) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **ABLE Account** | ABLE 账户 | 针对残障人士及其家庭的税收优惠储蓄账户（依据《获得更美好生活体验法》设立）。 | ABLE accounts allow people to invest after-tax dollars for disability expenses. |
| **Accredited Investor** | 合格投资者 | 符合特定收入、资产或职业执照（如Series 7）标准的投资者，在私募中不受数量限制。 | Regulation D allows private placements to be sold to an unlimited number of accredited investors. |
| **Accrued Interest** | 应计利息 | 两次付息日之间累计的利息。公司债/市政债按30/360计算，国债按实际天数计算。 | Investors may have to pay accrued interest when purchasing bonds in the market. |
| **Accumulation Units** | 累积单位 | 变动年金投入阶段的核算单位，类似于共同基金的股份。 | During the pay-in phase, an investor of a variable annuity purchases accumulation units. |
| **Ad Valorem Tax** | 从价税 | 基于财产价值征收的税（通常指财产税），是GO债券的主要担保来源。 | GO bonds are often backed by ad valorem taxes collected by the municipality. |
| **ADR (American Depositary Receipt)** | 美国存托凭证 | 代表外国股票所有权的凭证，在美国市场交易，通常由银行托管。 | ADRs are receipts for foreign securities traded in the United States. |
| **All or None (AON)** | 全额或取消 | 一种代销协议，要求承销商必须售出全部证券，否则取消发行。 | In an All or None offering, the deal is cancelled unless all capital is raised. |
| **American Style Option** | 美式期权 | 可以在到期日之前的任何时间行使的期权。 | American style options can be exercised any time up 'til expiration. |
| **Annuity** | 年金 | 保险公司提供的退休收入工具。分为固定年金和变动年金（证券）。 | Annuities are designed to provide supplemental retirement income. |
| **Annuity Units** | 年金单位 | 变动年金领取阶段的核算单位，其价值随投资表现波动。 | Accumulation units are converted into annuity units during the payout phase. |
| **Assumed Interest Rate (AIR)** | 假设利率 | 用于计算变动年金给付额调整的预期投资回报率基准。 | Payouts vary based on whether separate account performance exceeds the AIR. |
| **At-the-Money (ATM)** | 平值期权 | 行权价等于标的资产当前市场价格的状态。 | When the strike price is the same as the market price, the option is at-the-money. |
| **Authorized Shares** | 授权股份 | 公司章程规定的可以发行的最大股票数量。 | The corporate charter specifies the number of authorized shares. |
| **Basis Point** | 基点 | 衡量收益率的单位，1个基点等于0.01%。 | If a yield moves from 1.2% to 1.1%, it has moved ten basis points. |
| **Best Efforts Underwriting** | 代销 | 承销商承诺尽力销售但不承担未售出风险的承销方式。 | In a best efforts underwriting, the underwriter acts as an agent. |
| **Blue Sky Laws** | 蓝天法 | 美国各州的证券法规，要求证券及从业人员必须在州内注册。 | Issuers must register securities under state Blue Sky Laws. |
| **Bona Fide Offering** | 真实发行 | 按照公开发售价向公众诚实销售证券的行为。 | Firms must make a bona fide offering of securities at the public offering price. |
| **Bond Indenture** | 债券契约 | 发行人与持有人之间的法律协议，包含到期日、利率等核心条款。 | The indenture is the legal agreement between the issuer and bondholders. |
| **Breakpoint** | 费率折让点 | 共同基金对大额投资提供的销售费用折扣门槛。 | Breakpoints allow investors to receive a reduced sales charge for large purchases. |
| **Broker-Dealer** | 经纪交易商 | 从事证券买卖业务的公司，可作为代理人（Broker）或自营商（Dealer）。 | The issuer finds a broker-dealer to sell securities to the public. |
| **Build America Bonds (BABs)** | 建设美国债券 | 具有联邦补贴或税收抵免的应税市政债券。 | BABs were created to help municipalities raise money for infrastructure. |
| **Call Option** | 看涨期权 | 赋予持有人按固定价格买入标的资产权利的合约。 | A call option gives the holder the right to buy stock at a fixed price. |
| **Callable Preferred** | 可赎回优先股 | 发行人有权在特定日期后按预定价格回购的优先股。 | Callable preferred stock allows the issuer to buy back the shares. |
| **Closed-end Fund** | 封闭式基金 | 股份数量固定且在二级市场交易的投资公司。 | Closed-end funds act more like common stock in the secondary market. |
| **Commercial Paper** | 商业票据 | 期限在270天以内的短期无担保公司债务。 | Commercial paper is unsecured corporate debt with a maturity of 270 days or less. |
| **Common Stock** | 普通股 | 代表公司所有权的权益证券，具投票权和剩余索赔权。 | Corporations issue common stock to raise business capital. |
| **Competitive Offering** | 竞价发行 | 发行人通过投标程序选择承销商的发行方式。 | Municipal bonds are often sold through a competitive offering. |
| **Convertible Preferred** | 可转换优先股 | 允许持有人按预定比例转换为普通股的优先股。 | Convertible preferred allows investors to exchange for common stock. |
| **Cooling-off Period** | 冷却期 / 静默期 | 注册说明书提交后20天的等待期，期间仅限分发初步招股书。 | During the cooling-off period, money cannot be accepted for new issues. |
| **Coordination** | 协调注册 | 配合联邦注册同时进行的州级注册方式，常用于IPO。 | Coordination involves registering with the SEC and states at the same time. |
| **Coupon Rate** | 票面利率 | 债券面值上的固定年利率。 | The coupon rate on the bond determines the annual interest payment. |
| **Cumulative Preferred** | 累积优先股 | 若股息未支付，欠款会累积并优先于普通股补付。 | Cumulative preferred stockholders must be paid arrears before common dividends. |
| **Cumulative Voting** | 累积投票 | 允许股东集中投票权，旨在保护小股东利益。 | Cumulative voting gives smaller shareholders more representation flexibility. |
| **Current Yield (CY)** | 当期收益率 | 计算方式为：年利息 / 市场价格。 | Current yield reflects the return based on the bond's current price. |
| **Debentures** | 无抵押信用债券 | 仅凭发行人信誉担保的债务证券。 | Debentures are backed only by the issuer's good word and credit. |
| **Deficiency Letter** | 缺陷函 | SEC发出的要求修改注册说明书的通知。 | The SEC issues a deficiency letter if the registration statement needs amendments. |
| **Direct Participation Program (DPP)** | 直接参与计划 | 将收入和亏损直接穿透给投资者的合伙制商业计划。 | DPPs pass through income and losses directly to the investors. |
| **Dividend** | 股息 / 红利 | 公司向股东分配的利润。 | Cash dividends are a way for a corporation to share its profits. |
| **Due Diligence Meeting** | 尽职调查会议 | 承销商在冷却期结束前举行的关于发行的审查会议。 | The lead underwriter holds a due-diligence meeting to share information about the issue. |
| **Effective Date** | 生效日 | SEC允许证券开始正式向公众销售的日期。 | Underwriters can sell the securities once the effective date is reached. |
| **Equity REIT** | 权益型 REIT | 投资并直接经营房产的信托基金，主要收益来自租金。 | Equity REITs derive income from rent and property sales. |
| **Escrow Account** | 代管账户 | 用于暂时存放代销发行中投资者资金的账户。 | Purchasers' money is held in an escrow account until the deal terms are met. |
| **European Style Option** | 欧式期权 | 仅在到期日当天可以行使的期权。 | European-style options can only be exercised on the expiration date. |
| **Exchange-Traded Fund (ETF)** | 交易所交易基金 | 在交易所上市交易、通常追踪指数的投资基金。 | ETFs offer diversification with the ease of stock trading. |
| **Exchange-Traded Note (ETN)** | 交易所交易票据 | 与指数挂钩的无担保债务工具。 | ETNs are debt securities issued by financial institutions. |
| **Exempt Securities** | 豁免证券 | 无需在SEC注册即可发行的证券（如国债、市政债）。 | U.S. government securities are exempt from registration requirements. |
| **Exempt Transactions** | 豁免交易 | 无需注册的特定交易（如私募、州内发行）。 | Regulation D is a common exempt transaction for private placements. |
| **Face-amount Certificate** | 面值证单 | 承诺未来支付固定面值的投资合约。 | A face-amount certificate is a type of packaged security. |
| **Final Prospectus** | 最终招股说明书 | 包含最终定价等完整信息的法定销售文件。 | Purchasers must receive a final prospectus at or before settlement. |
| **Firm Commitment** | 全额包销 | 承销商买入全部证券并承担销售风险的承销方式。 | In firm commitment, the underwriter assumes all financial risk. |
| **Fixed Annuity** | 固定年金 | 保险公司保证固定收益率的非证券年金产品。 | Fixed annuities are exempt from SEC registration. |
| **Funded Debt** | 长期债务 | 期限通常在一年以上的公司债务。 | Funded debt refers to corporate liabilities with a maturity of more than one year. |
| **General Obligation (GO) Bond** | 一般责任债券 | 由发行人完全信誉和征税权担保的市政债券。 | GO bonds fund non-revenue projects like schools and roads. |
| **Hybrid REIT** | 混合型 REIT | 兼具权益型和抵押型特征的REIT。 | Hybrid REITs invest in both properties and mortgages. |
| **In-the-Money (ITM)** | 实值期权 | 期权行权价优于当前市价，具有内在价值。 | A call is in-the-money if the market price exceeds the strike price. |
| **Indication of Interest (IOI)** | 投资意向 | 投资者在冷却期表达的购买意向，无法律约束力。 | Underwriters obtain indications of interest during the cooling-off period. |
| **Intrastate Offering** | 州内发行 | 依据Rule 147在单一州内进行的豁免注册发行。 | Rule 147 limits the offering to residents of a single state. |
| **Intrinsic Value** | 内在价值 | 期权处于实值状态的溢价部分。 | Intrinsic value is the amount by which an option is in-the-money. |
| **Investment Banking Firm** | 投资银行 | 协助发行人筹集资金并提供并购建议的金融机构。 | Investment banking firms help issuers raise money. |
| **Investment Company Act of 1940** | 1940年投资公司法 | 规范共同基金等投资公司的基本法律。 | Investment companies are regulated by the Act of 1940. |
| **Issuer** | 发行人 | 筹集资金并承担债务或发行股份的实体。 | The issuer is the entity raising capital through securities. |
| **Junk Bonds** | 垃圾债券 / 高收益债券 | 信用评级在投资级以下的投机级债券。 | High-yield bonds are riskier but offer higher potential returns. |
| **Letter of Intent (LOI)** | 意向书 | 承诺未来投资以获得折让费率的非约束性声明。 | An LOI allows investors to hit a breakpoint over 13 months. |
| **Management Investment Company** | 管理型投资公司 | 聘请基金经理管理投资组合的公司（含开放式和封闭式）。 | Management companies actively or passively manage portfolios. |
| **Managing Underwriter** | 管理承销商 | 负责组建承销团并与发行人直接对接的主承销商。 | The managing underwriter receives financial compensation for every share sold. |
| **Maturity Date** | 到期日 | 债务证券本金必须偿还的日期。 | The maturity date is when the principal amount is paid back. |
| **Mini-Max** | 最小-最大 | 设定了最低生效门槛和最高发行限额的代销方式。 | A Mini-Max offering requires a minimum floor to proceed. |
| **Money Market Fund** | 货币市场基金 | 投资于短期、高安全性债务工具的共同基金。 | Money market funds maintain a stable NAV, typically $1. |
| **Mortgage REIT** | 抵押型 REIT | 投资于房地产抵押贷款及相关证券的REIT。 | Mortgage REITs earn income from interest on real estate loans. |
| **Municipal Bond** | 市政债券 | 州、市或地方政府发行的债务证券。 | Municipal bonds usually offer federally tax-free interest. |
| **Mutual Fund (Open-end Fund)** | 共同基金 / 开放式基金 | 持续发行且可随时赎回的投资公司。 | Mutual funds provide liquidity and professional management. |
| **Negotiated Offering** | 议价发行 | 发行人直接选择承销商并协商发行条款的发行方式。 | Corporate issues are typically sold through a negotiated offering. |
| **Net Asset Value (NAV)** | 基金净值 | 资产减负债除以股数，是开放式基金的定价基础。 | NAV is computed at least once every business day for mutual funds. |
| **NMS Securities** | NMS证券 | 在全国市场系统（交易所/纳斯达克）交易的证券。 | A final prospectus must be available for 25 days for IPOs of NMS securities. |
| **No Approval Clause** | 不批准条款 | 声明SEC不批准或不担保发行质量的法律条款。 | It is unlawful to represent that the SEC approved an issue (No Approval Clause). |
| **Notification (Registration by Filing)** | 通知注册（备案注册） | 针对成熟发行人的简化州级注册方式。 | Notification is the simplest form of state registration for established companies. |
| **Options Clearing Corp (OCC)** | 期权结算公司 | 所有上市期权的发行人和结算中心。 | The OCC guarantees the performance of option contracts. |
| **Out-of-the-Money (OTM)** | 虚值期权 | 行使该期权在当前市价下无利可图的状态。 | An option is out-of-the-money if it has zero intrinsic value. |
| **Outstanding Shares** | 流通股数 | 投资者持有的已发行股数总和。 | Outstanding shares = Issued shares - Treasury stock. |
| **Par Value** | 面值 | 证券票面名义价值，债券通常为$1,000。 | Par value is the denomination of the bond or stock. |
| **Passive Income/Loss** | 被动收入/损失 | 依据税务规则，来自不直接参与管理之投资的盈亏。 | Passive losses can only offset passive income. |
| **Preferred Stock** | 优先股 | 具有优先派息权和优先清算权的权益证券。 | Preferred stock is senior to common stock regarding dividends. |
| **Preliminary Prospectus** | 初步招股说明书 | 静默期使用的披露文件，又称“红鲱鱼”。 | The preliminary prospectus is used to solicit indications of interest. |
| **Private Placement** | 私募发行 | 面向少数合格投资者的非公开发行。 | Private placements are often sold under Regulation D. |
| **Proxy** | 代理权 / 委托书 | 股东授权他人代为执行投票权的凭证。 | Shareholders can vote by proxy if they cannot attend meetings. |
| **Public Offering Price (POP)** | 公开发售价 | 投资者购买开放式基金的价格（NAV + 销售费）。 | The POP is the price paid by the customer for fund shares. |
| **Put Option** | 看跌期权 | 赋予持有人按固定价格卖出标的资产权利的合约。 | A put option allows the holder to sell shares at a set price. |
| **Qualification** | 核准注册 | 需州管理机构审查批准的州级注册方式，用于非豁免联邦注册证券。 | Qualification is used for securities exempt from SEC registration but requiring state registration. |
| **Qualified Institutional Buyer (QIB)** | 合格机构买家 | 管理至少$1亿资产的机构（如保险公司、基金）。 | Rule 144A allows restricted securities to be traded among QIBs. |
| **Real Estate Investment Trust (REIT)** | 不动产投资信托 | 投资于房产或抵押贷款的税收优待投资工具。 | REITs must distribute 90% of income to avoid corporate tax. |
| **Red Herring** | 红鲱鱼 | 初步招股说明书的俗称，因封面有红色免责声明而得名。 | A preliminary prospectus is sometimes called a red herring. |
| **Registered Representative** | 登记代表 | 在FINRA注册并代表经纪交易商从事业务的个人。 | Registered reps can work for broker-dealers or investment banking firms. |
| **Registrar** | 登记代理人 | 负责监控发行在外股份数量不超过授权股数的机构。 | The registrar ensures outstanding shares don't exceed authorized shares. |
| **Registration Statement** | 注册说明书 | 提交给SEC的关于新发行的详细披露文件（S-1）。 | Issuers must file a registration statement for non-exempt securities. |
| **Regulation A (Reg A+)** | A条例 | 针对小额发行的简化注册豁免。 | Regulation A+ allows small businesses to raise up to $75 million. |
| **Regulation D** | D条例 | 私募发行的注册豁免条款。 | Regulation D governs private securities offerings. |
| **Restricted Stock** | 受限股票 | 通过非公开交易获得、转售受到限制的股票。 | Restricted stock usually has a 6-month holding period (Rule 144). |
| **Revenue Bond** | 收益债券 | 由特定项目收入担保的市政债券。 | Revenue bonds fund self-supporting projects like toll roads. |
| **Rights** | 配股权 | 现有股东维持比例认购新股的短期、折扣价权利。 | Preemptive rights allow shareholders to maintain their ownership %. |
| **Rule 144** | 144规则 | 规定了受限股和控制股在公开市场转售的条件。 | Rule 144 sets limits on volume and holding periods. |
| **Rule 144A** | 144A规则 | 允许QIBs之间自由交易未注册的私募证券。 | Rule 144A enhances liquidity for private placement securities. |
| **SEC** | 证券交易委员会 | 美国联邦主要的证券监管机构。 | The SEC oversees the U.S. financial markets. |
| **Secondary Offering** | 二级发售 | 现有股东出售所持股份，收益归股东而非公司。 | In a secondary offering, no new shares are created. |
| **Selling Group** | 销售小组 | 协助承销团销售但不承担包销风险的经纪商。 | Selling group members help distribute shares but have no financial commitment. |
| **Separate Account** | 独立账户 | 存放变动年金投资资产且与保险公司总账分开的账户。 | Variable annuity premiums are invested in a separate account. |
| **Serial Bond** | 分期偿还债券 | 同一批次中具有不同到期日的债券。 | Municipal projects are often funded with serial bond issues. |
| **Shelf Registration** | 暂搁注册 / 货架注册 | 允许发行人在三年内分批出售已注册证券（Rule 415）。 | Shelf registration offers issuers flexibility in timing their sale. |
| **Sinking Fund** | 偿债基金 | 为未来偿还本金而设立的专户资金。 | A sinking fund reduces the risk of default at maturity. |
| **Split Offering (Combined Offering)** | 组合发行 | 同时包含一级发售和二级发售的证券发行。 | A split offering involves both new and outstanding securities. |
| **Spread** | 价差 / 承销差价 | 发行价与发行人到手价之间的差额，是承销商的利润。 | The underwriting spread is the difference between POP and net price. |
| **Standby Underwriting** | 备用承销 | 承销商承诺购买配股发行中股东未认购股份的承销方式。 | A standby underwriter agrees to purchase any stock not purchased by the public. |
| **Statement of Additional Information (SAI)** | 附加信息陈述书 | 共同基金招股书的补充文件（B部分）。 | Mutual funds may provide a Statement of Additional Information for more details. |
| **Statutory Voting** | 法定投票 | 每股在一席位上一票的常规投票制。 | Statutory voting favors larger shareholders over smaller ones. |
| **Stock Split** | 股票拆分 | 改变总股数而不改变总市值的行为。 | A 2-for-1 stock split doubles the shares and halves the price. |
| **Stop Order** | 停止令 | SEC中止注册说明书生效的命令。 | The SEC issues a stop order to suspend the effectiveness of a registration statement. |
| **Syndicate** | 承销团 | 为分担大型发行风险而组成的承销商联盟。 | The syndicate shares the financial risk of an underwriting. |
| **Syndicate Manager** | 承销团经理 | 负责组建承销团并管理发行的主承销商。 | The syndicate manager forms the syndicate and deals with the issuer. |
| **Taxable Equivalent Yield (TEY)** | 税收等值收益率 | 计算对比免税市政债与应税公司债收益率的公式。 | TEY = Municipal Yield / (100% - Tax Bracket). |
| **Tender Offer** | 要约收购 | 向所有股东发出的溢价收购股份的公开提议。 | A company may initiate a tender offer to buy back its shares. |
| **Term Bond** | 到期债券 / 定期债券 | 同一批次中在同一日期全部到期的债券。 | Term bonds mature all at once on a single date. |
| **Time Value** | 时间价值 | 权利金扣除内在价值后的剩余部分。 | Time value decreases as the option approaches expiration. |
| **Tombstone Advertisement** | 墓碑广告 | 冷却期内唯一允许的简单发行公告。 | A tombstone ad is the only advertisement allowed during the cooling-off period. |
| **Transfer Agent** | 过户代理人 | 负责维护股东记录、过户、发证和派息的机构。 | The transfer agent issues certificates and distributes dividends. |
| **Treasury Stock** | 库存股 | 发行人已发行但又回购回来的股份，无投票权和股息。 | Treasury stock is excluded from outstanding share counts. |
| **Triple Tax-Free** | 三重免税 | 免除联邦、州、地方三级税收（通常指美国领地发行的债券）。 | Bonds from Puerto Rico are triple tax-free for all U.S. residents. |
| **Underwriting Agreement** | 承销协议 | 发行人与承销商之间签署的规定发行条款的合同。 | The underwriting agreement is signed before securities can be sold. |
| **Unfunded Debt** | 短期债务 | 期限在一年以内的短期债务。 | Unfunded debt consists of short-term liabilities. |
| **Unit Investment Trust (UIT)** | 单位投资信托 | 投资于固定投资组合且无主动管理的投资公司。 | UITs have no investment adviser and low ongoing fees. |
| **Variable Annuity** | 变动年金 | 具有投资风险且被视为证券的年金产品。 | Variable annuities require both a securities and insurance license. |
| **Warrants** | 认股权证 | 赋予长期、溢价购买股票权利的证券，通常作为融资优惠。 | Warrants are long-term instruments used as sweeteners. |
| **Yield to Maturity (YTM)** | 到期收益率 | 衡量投资者持有债券至到期的综合回报率（基准）。 | YTM is the most comprehensive measure of a bond's yield. |

## 常见缩写 (Abbreviations)
- **IPO (Initial Public Offering)**: 首次公开募股
- **APO (Additional Public Offering)**: 增发 / 后续公开发行
- **FINRA**: 美国金融业监管局
- **MSRB**: 市政证券规则制定委员会
- **OTC**: 场外交易市场
- **NYSE**: 纽约证券交易所
- **DTCC**: 美国证券存托结算公司
- **T+1**: 交易日后一个营业日结算（2024年5月起执行的标准）
| **Issued Shares** | 已发行股份 | 发行人为筹集资金而实际向公众出售的那部分授权股份。 | Issued shares are the portion of authorized shares that the issuer has actually sold. |
| **Reverse Stock Split** | 反向拆股 | 减少股份数量并提高股价的公司行为。 | A company may reverse-split its stock if the market price gets too low. |
| **Ex-dividend Date** | 除息日 | 股票在不含股息的情况下交易的第一天。 | The market price of the stock falls on the ex-dividend date. |
| **Stock Dividend** | 股票股息 | 公司以额外股份形式向股东派发的股息。 | Stock dividends are just like forward stock splits in that the investor receives more shares. |
| **Limited Liability** | 有限责任 | 股东的责任仅限于其投资金额的法律概念。 | Stockholders have limited liability, meaning they only lose what they invested. |
| **Trustee** | 受托人 | 为机构管理债券发行并确保发行人履行义务的组织。 | The bond indenture includes the name of a trustee. |
| **Series Bonds** | 系列债券 | 在连续年份分批发行但只有一个到期日的债券。 | Construction companies are most likely to issue series bonds. |
| **Mortgage Bond** | 抵押债券 | 由发行人拥有的房产提供担保的债券。 | Mortgage bonds are backed by property that the issuer owns. |
| **Equipment Trust** | 设备信托 | 由运输公司发行并由其设备（如飞机、卡车）担保的债券。 | Equipment trusts are backed by equipment they own. |
| **Collateral Trust Bond** | 抵押信托债券 | 由发行人持有的金融资产（如股票、债券）担保的债券。 | Collateral trust bonds are backed by financial assets. |
| **Guaranteed Bond** | 担保债券 | 由原始发行人以外的公司（通常是母公司）担保的债券。 | If the issuer defaults on a guaranteed bond, the guarantor pays off the bonds. |
| **Income Bond** | 收益债券 | 仅在发行人收入足够高时才支付利息的债券，通常由重组中的公司发行。 | Income bonds are considered to be the riskiest of all. |
| **Yield to Call (YTC)** | 赎回收益率 | 假设债券在最早赎回日被赎回时的收益率。 | The yield to call is the amount the investor receives if the bond is called early. |
| **Yield to Worst (YTW)** | 最差收益率 | 所有可能赎回日期的到期收益率和赎回收益率中的最低值。 | To determine the yield to worst, choose the lowest of YTM and YTCs. |
| **Total Return** | 总回报 | 特定时期内投资的全部回报（包括利息/股息和资本利得）。 | The total return calculates the full return on a particular investment. |
| **Repurchase Agreement (Repo)** | 回购协议 | 卖方同意在未来特定时间以特定价格买回证券的短期合同。 | Repos are short-term loans involving the sale and repurchase of securities. |
| **Federal Funds** | 联邦基金 | 银行间为满足准备金要求而进行的隔夜贷款。 | Federal funds are usually overnight loans between banks. |
| **Banker's Acceptance (BA)** | 银行承兑汇票 | 由银行保证支付的定期汇票，常用于进出口贸易融资。 | Companies use BAs for importing and exporting goods. |
| **Eurodollar** | 欧洲美元 | 存放在美国境外银行的美元存款。 | Eurodollars are American dollars held by a foreign bank. |
| **Brokered CD** | 经纪商存单 | 由银行发行但通过经纪商向投资者出售的低风险存单，可在二级市场交易。 | Brokered CDs can be traded in the market unlike typical CDs. |
| **Treasury Bill (T-bill)** | 短期国库券 | 期限为1年或以内的美国政府折价债务证券。 | T-bills are issued at a discount and mature at par. |
| **Treasury Note (T-note)** | 中期国库票据 | 期限为2至10年的美国政府债务证券，每半年付息。 | Treasury notes pay interest every 6 months. |
| **Treasury Bond (T-bond)** | 长期国债 | 期限为10年以上（通常20或30年）的美国政府债务证券。 | Treasury bonds have maturities of up to 30 years. |
| **TIPS** | 通胀保护证券 | 面值随通胀调整的美国国库证券。 | The par value of TIPS adjusts according to inflation. |
| **T-STRIPS** | 零息国债 | 本息分离的零息美国政府证券。 | T-STRIPS are considered to be zero-coupon securities. |
| **Agency Bond** | 机构债券 | 由美国政府赞助机构（GSE）发行的债券。 | Agency bonds are backed by the U.S. government but carry slightly more risk. |
| **GNMA (Ginnie Mae)** | 政府全国抵押贷款协会 | 唯一由美国政府完全信誉担保的住房抵押贷款证券发行机构。 | GNMAs are backed by the full faith and credit of the U.S. government. |
| **Industrial Development Revenue Bond (IDR)** | 工业发展收益债券 | 由市政府发行但由私人公司的租赁付款担保的债券。 | IDRs are risky because they are backed by the corporation, not the municipality. |
| **Official Statement** | 官方声明书 | 市政债券发行的披露文件，类似于公司证券的招股说明书。 | The official statement provides detailed information about the municipal bond issue. |
| **Build America Bond (BAB)** | 建设美国债券 | 应税市政债券，发行人可获得联邦补贴或投资者获得税收抵免。 | BABs were created to assist municipalities in raising funds for infrastructure. |
| **Tax Anticipation Note (TAN)** | 税收预期票据 | 为平滑税收流入不均而发行的短期市政债务。 | Municipalities issue TANs to finance current operations in anticipation of future tax receipts. |
| **529 Savings Plan** | 529 储蓄计划 | 一种税收优惠的教育储蓄计划。 | 529 plans allow for tax-free withdrawals for qualified education expenses. |
| **Face-amount Certificate Company** | 面值证单公司 | 发行以折扣价出售并按面值到期的债务凭证的投资公司。 | Face-amount certificate companies are rare today. |
| **Sales Charge (Load)** | 销售费用 / 申购费 | 投资者购买或赎回共同基金时支付的佣金。 | No-load funds do not charge a sales load. |
| **12b-1 Fee** | 12b-1 费用 | 共同基金每年收取的用于营销和分销的费用。 | 12b-1 fees are deducted from the fund's assets. |
| **Rights of Accumulation (ROA)** | 累计优先权 | 允许投资者结合现有持仓和新投资来达到费率折让点。 | Rights of accumulation allow for reduced sales charges on subsequent purchases. |
| **Limited Partnership (LP)** | 有限合伙 | 由至少一名普通合伙人和一名有限合伙人组成的商业组织形式。 | Limited partnerships are the most common form of DPP. |
| **General Partner (GP)** | 普通合伙人 | 负责管理合伙企业并承担无限责任的合伙人。 | The GP makes the day-to-day decisions for the partnership. |
| **Limited Partner (LP)** | 有限合伙人 | 仅提供资本、不参与管理且承担有限责任的合伙人。 | Limited partners have liability limited to their investment. |
